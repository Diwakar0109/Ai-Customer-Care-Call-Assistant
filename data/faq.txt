Q: What is your return policy?
A: You can initiate a return for any item within 7 days of its delivery. To start the process, please visit the returns portal on our website and enter your tracking ID and reason for return. The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

Q: How long does standard delivery take to major cities?
A: Standard delivery to major cities like Bangalore, Chennai, Mumbai, or Delhi typically takes 3 to 5 business days. For other locations, it might take up to 7 business days.

Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
A: You can change the delivery address only if the parcel has not yet been dispatched from our main warehouse. Please call our customer support hotline immediately with your tracking ID to check the status and request the change. Once dispatched, the address cannot be changed.

Q: What should I do if my package is marked as "Delivered" but I haven't received it?
A: First, please check with your family members, neighbors, or the security office of your building, as they might have collected it on your behalf. If you still cannot locate the parcel after 24 hours, contact us, and we will launch a formal investigation with the courier partner.

Q: Are there any extra charges for Cash on Delivery (COD)?
A: Yes, there is a nominal handling fee of 40 rupees for all Cash on Delivery orders. This fee is non-refundable.

Q: What are your customer service hours?
A: Our customer service team is available via phone and chat from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM, Monday through Saturday. We are closed on Sundays and national holidays.

Q: What happens if I miss my delivery?
A: The delivery agent will attempt to deliver your package a total of three times. If all three attempts are unsuccessful, the package will be returned to our warehouse. You will then need to contact us to arrange for a re-shipment, which may incur additional charges.

Q: How do I cancel my order?
A: You can cancel your order directly from your account on our website, but only if the item has not yet been shipped. If the "Cancel Order" button is not visible, it means the item is already on its way and cannot be cancelled. In that case, you may refuse the delivery.

Q: Do you offer international shipping?
A: Currently, we only deliver within India. We do not offer international shipping at this time, but we are working on expanding our services in the future.
